Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Piegames ("we") makes and operates the mobile game MagicStone: Pilgrimage (the "Game"). This policy explains what information the Game handles and how.

1. No accounts

The Game has no sign-up or login. We do not receive information that directly identifies you, such as your name, phone number or address. Your progress (cleared boards, Moonstone count, etc.) is stored only on your device.

2. What the Game handles and why

3. Your choices

  • Ad and analytics consent: In regions that require it, a consent form appears when mana first appears. You can change your choice later in Settings › Ad privacy (shown only where required).
  • iOS tracking: You can decline in the iOS "Allow app to track" prompt and change it at any time in Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking. The Game works the same either way.
  • Advertising ID: You can reset or delete it in Android Settings › Privacy › Ads.
  • Game data on your device: Uninstalling the Game removes the progress and transaction IDs stored on your device. Deleted progress cannot be restored.

4. Retention

  • Analytics events are kept for 14 months and crash reports for 90 days, as set in Google's services.
  • Support emails are kept for one year after we reply, then deleted.

5. Third parties and international transfers

The processors above (Google LLC, Apple Inc.) may process information outside your country, including in the United States. Their policies: Google · Google advertising · Firebase · Apple. We do not sell your information.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect information from children under 13. If we learn that we have, we will delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may ask to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of information we hold. Because we hold almost no information that identifies you directly, we may need your device's app instance ID to find it. Contact us at the address below.

8. Contact

9. Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with its date and announce important changes in the Game.
